Trend 6 · Motion & animation

Motion that guides, never rushes.

Figma's 2026 trend: motion adds rhythm and storytelling — from subtle hover states to full scroll-based narratives — guiding attention and building immersion without slowing performance. The rule that matters most: match the easing to the brand's tone. A Tamil home church reads smooth, slow, reverent — so CTC uses long, soft ease-outs. No bounce, no spring, no restless loops.

01 · Micro — hover & press
Plan a visit

Lift −2px · 180ms ease. Button ripple originates at the click point — an animated state that confirms the tap.

02 · Card lift & image warm
Sunday School
Hover me

Card rises −4px, image scales 1.04 & warms (saturate 1.08) over 500ms.

03 · List — animated focus
iWorship
iiFellowship

The hovered row indents and turns italic-green — light moving to where you look.

04 · Easing curve · the CTC signature
cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1)

A long, gentle ease-out. Fast to start, soft to settle — reverent, never snappy. 700–900ms on entrances.

05 · Scroll-triggered reveal

Matthew 11:28

Content rises 22px + fades as it enters view — small stagger, once, never on loop. Replay ↺
