# Homepage Guidelines — Quiet Grace Minimal

## Purpose

The Christ Tamil Church homepage should act as a warm digital front door. It should quickly help a first-time visitor understand:

- Who Christ Tamil Church is
- When Sunday worship happens
- Where the church is located
- How to plan a visit
- How to watch online
- How families and children can connect
- What events and ministries are available
- How to access sermons and contact the church

The homepage should not try to show everything. It should guide people deeper into the site.

## Homepage Principles

### 1. Spacious, Not Cramped

Use generous whitespace. Do not compress every piece of content into the top half of the page.

### 2. Fewer Cards

Cards are useful, but too many cards make the site feel like a dashboard. Use cards only when they help scanning.

### 3. Clear Visitor Journey

A visitor should be able to answer these questions within a few seconds:

- What kind of church is this?
- When is worship?
- Where is it?
- How do I visit?
- Can I watch online?

### 4. Warm Human Photography

Use one strong image per major section. Avoid collages and busy image grids.

### 5. Section Rhythm

Use subtle background changes:

- Hero: warm white
- Service highlight: white card
- Welcome: soft canvas
- Events: light mist
- Ministries: warm white
- Sermons: white
- Visitor section: soft peach
- Footer: deep ink

## Recommended Homepage Sections

1. Sticky navigation
2. Hero
3. Service essentials band
4. Welcome/community section
5. Upcoming events preview
6. Ministries preview
7. Latest sermon/watch online
8. First-time visitor section
9. Footer

## Hero Section

### Goal

Communicate the identity and warmth of the church immediately.

### Content

Small label:

```text
WELCOME TO CHRIST TAMIL CHURCH
```

Headline:

```text
A Tamil church family rooted in Christ, love, and community
```

Supporting text:

```text
Join us for worship, God’s Word, prayer, and fellowship as we grow together as one family in Christ.
```

Identity line:

```text
Rooted in Christ. United in Love. Sent to Serve.
```

Buttons:

- Plan Your Visit
- Watch Online

### Visual

Use one warm, high-quality image. Avoid a collage.

Best image themes:

- Tamil/South Asian family at church entrance
- Church family after worship
- Welcoming fellowship moment
- Natural light and authentic smiles

### Floating Card

Use only one floating card:

```text
Sunday Worship
10:30 AM
1330 63rd St,
Downers Grove, IL
Get Directions
```

## Service Essentials Band

Use one wide white card with four columns:

1. Sunday Worship — 10:30 AM, Every Sunday
2. Location — 1330 63rd St, Downers Grove, IL
3. Families Welcome — A place for every age to belong and grow
4. Word & Prayer — Biblical teaching and Spirit-led worship

Keep icons simple and minimal.

## Welcome / Community Section

### Background

Soft Canvas `#F1EEE7`

### Layout

Two columns:

- Image on one side
- Text on the other

### Heading

```text
A place to belong.
A family to grow with.
```

### Copy

```text
At Christ Tamil Church, we are a multi-generational family passionate about Jesus and committed to loving one another. Whether you’re new to faith or looking for a church home, you’ll find a place to belong here.
```

### Chips

- Christ-centered worship
- Tamil Christian fellowship
- Family & community

## Upcoming Events Section

### Background

Light Mist `#E7E5DE`

### Homepage Rule

Show only 3 events on the homepage.

Recommended examples:

- Church Family Picnic
- Youth Worship Night
- Women’s Fellowship

Use a `View All Events` link to send users to the full events page.

### Card Content

Each event card should include:

- Image
- Date badge
- Event title
- Time
- Location
- Optional CTA

Avoid showing 4–6 event cards on the homepage.

## Ministries Preview Section

### Homepage Rule

Show only 4 ministries:

- Sunday School
- Kids Circle
- Bible Study
- Prayer

Add a `View All Ministries` link.

The full ministries list can live on the Grow/Ministries page.

## Latest Sermon Section

Use one horizontal media card.

Content:

- Thumbnail with play button
- Label: Latest Sermon
- Title: Walking in Faith
- Subtitle: Trusting God in Every Season
- Pastor/date line
- Short description
- Watch Online button
- Browse Sermons button

Do not show a sermon grid on the homepage.

## First-Time Visitor Section

### Background

Soft Peach `#F3D4C2` or Soft Canvas.

### Heading

```text
Visiting for the first time?
```

### Copy

```text
We’d love to meet you. Here’s what you can expect when you visit.
```

### Steps

1. Arrive — Come as you are. We’ll welcome you.
2. Worship — Experience uplifting worship and biblical teaching.
3. Connect — Meet our team and find your place.

CTA:

- Plan Your Visit

## Footer

### Background

Deep Ink `#1F2320`

### Content

Include:

- Church name/logo
- Tagline: Rooted in Christ. United in Love. Sent to Serve.
- Sunday Worship: 10:30 AM
- Address
- Quick links
- Contact email/phone
- Social icons

Keep footer spacious and not crowded.

## Homepage Content Priority

Priority order:

1. Church identity
2. Sunday worship time
3. Location
4. Plan Your Visit
5. Watch Online
6. Welcome/community identity
7. Upcoming events
8. Ministries preview
9. Latest sermon
10. First-time visitor reassurance

## Homepage Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Four or more event cards above the fold
- Eight ministry cards on the homepage
- Busy hero image collages
- More than one floating card on hero
- Large dark hero section
- Too many icons in a row
- Dense dashboard-like sections
- Repeating the same card style everywhere
- Overusing beige, green, or gold
