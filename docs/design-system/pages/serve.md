# Serve page

The Serve page follows the CTC inner-page blueprint and uses the live homepage
as its visual reference.

- Light editorial hero with Tamil-first kicker, Fraunces display type, one
  bronze contact CTA, and a warm community image.
- Sage story band preserves the legacy Community Outreach message and its
  Saturday-before-Communion-Sunday rhythm, including the Feed My Starving
  Children reference and Proverbs 22:9.
- Editorial row list presents food packing, meal service, and donation drives
  without a dense card-grid treatment.
- Page-local motion is limited to gentle reveal transitions and fully respects
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.
