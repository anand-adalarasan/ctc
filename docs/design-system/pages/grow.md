# Page design: Grow (`/grow`)

The Grow hub is the entry point to discipleship at Christ Tamil Church. It uses an editorial image hero, a scannable row index, alternating sage and cream bands, Scripture, and one clear closing invitation.

The pathways are Bible Study & Prayer, Sunday School (B.L.A.S.T.), Kids Circle, and Sermons. Kids Circle is a brief experience during worship; B.L.A.S.T. is the structured Sunday School experience. Motion is limited to the sitewide reduced-motion-safe reveal pattern.

Responsive behavior follows the established 1120px, 900px, and 620px breakpoints. Rows collapse without hiding descriptions and calls to action retain accessible touch targets.
