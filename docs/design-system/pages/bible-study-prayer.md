# Page design: Bible Study & Prayer (`/grow/bible-study-prayer`)

This page presents adult discipleship as an approachable rhythm of Scripture, prayer, fellowship, and everyday formation. Its Tamil-first image hero, live schedule card, editorial gathering rows, image-led expectations band, Scripture feature, related pathways, and closing invitation follow the CTC inner-page blueprint.

Specific times should come from confirmed shared data. The page uses canonical tokens, semantic headings, and reduced-motion-safe reveals.
