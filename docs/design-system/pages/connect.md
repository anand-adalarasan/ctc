# Page design: Connect (`/connect`)

Connect presents fellowship as shared life rather than a directory of programs. The hero gives first-time visitors the Sunday rhythm immediately, followed by a warm community image, an editorial list of annual traditions, a pastoral care band, and one clear closing invitation.

The content expands the church’s established Connect activities: Sunday fellowship meals, summer picnic, family visits, summer carnival, harvest festival, and carol rounds. The legacy Fellowship Hour page informed the auditorium and after-worship details; its older clock time is intentionally omitted in favor of the current shared worship schedule.

Visual styling follows the CTC inner-page template: green-led typography, restrained brass accents, alternating white/cream/sage bands, one bronze primary CTA per view, and no homepage-exclusive atmospheric effects. Motion is limited to the reduced-motion-safe reveal pattern. Responsive behavior follows the established 1120px, 900px, and 620px breakpoints.
