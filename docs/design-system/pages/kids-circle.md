# Page design: Kids Circle (`/grow/kids-circle`)

Kids Circle is positioned as a brief, joyful children’s teaching moment during Sunday worship. Its isolated `kc-*` layout uses a Tamil-first image hero, editorial experience rows, parent reassurance band, B.L.A.S.T. relationship, Scripture feature, and related family pathways.

The treatment remains within the premium CTC system rather than creating a separate cartoon children’s brand: warm cream and sage bands, restrained Lucide icons, editorial typography, and canonical tokens.
